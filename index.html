01 // ABOUT ME

Background & Dual Technical Foundation

My hands-on technical instinct began in the workshop at Car Cosmetics. Working on vehicles taught me the fundamentals of mechanical and electrical troubleshooting: interpreting live FORScan diagnostic data, scanning OBD-II fault codes, bleeding hydraulic brake systems to exact tolerances, and isolating physical mechanical failures.

Now based in Preston, I study Electrical & Mechanical Engineering at Lancashire University (UCLan). This degree unites hands-on workshop experience with rigorous circuit analysis, electromagnetic physics, thermodynamics, instrumentation, and electromechanical systems design.

In parallel, I have built an extensive background in Computer Science & Information Communications Technology (ICT)—developing an in-depth understanding of system architectures, low-level computing, networking, and digital tooling. Adding to this multi-disciplinary profile, my time as a former DJ developed my practical understanding of live sound engineering, signal routing, mixer telemetry, and crowd engagement under pressure.
