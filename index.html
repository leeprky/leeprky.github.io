01 // ABOUT ME

Technical Profile & Dual Foundation

I believe the most effective engineers are those who know what it’s like to hold a wrench, isolate a stubborn CAN Bus error code, and bleed a brake caliper until there isn’t a single bubble left in the fluid line. My foundation was built with grease and diagnostic tools at Car Cosmetics, working with FORScan and OBD-II interfaces across everyday vehicles and performance builds.

Moving to Preston to study Electrical & Mechanical Engineering at Lancashire University (UCLan) allowed me to bridge that physical workshop knowledge with higher-level mathematical modeling, circuit analysis, thermodynamics, and electromechanical systems.

Alongside engineering, I have a deep history in software styling and community UI design—building well-known Discord client themes (like WindowsXI, MaterialYou, and Vintage) using deep custom CSS layout engines, and commanding dancefloors with electronic sets as DJ Leethal.
