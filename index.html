BIOGRAPHY & ENGINEERING JOURNEY

About Me

I am an undergraduate student studying Engineering at Lancashire University (UCLan), combining academic engineering theory with practical hands-on mechanics and low-level software engineering.

My technical path began under the hoods of vehicles during my automotive technician apprenticeship at Car Cosmetics in Blackpool, utilizing FORScan and OBD-II telemetry to diagnose complex ECU sensor interactions and repair mechanical systems. That fascination with real-time feedback and machine-level mechanics naturally extended into software: writing clean-room desktop engines in pure Rust, customizing Android Linux kernels with KernelSU and SUSFS root sandbox isolation, and designing community-loved UI themes with over 100+ GitHub stars.
