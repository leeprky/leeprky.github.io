01 // ABOUT ME

Practical Foundations & Electrical Engineering

My hands-on technical foundation began in the automotive workshop at Car Cosmetics. Working on vehicles taught me practical mechanical troubleshooting: inspecting vehicle wiring, diagnosing electrical and sensor faults, bleeding hydraulic brake systems to exact specifications, and overhauling mechanical components.

Now based in Preston, I study Electrical Engineering at Lancashire University (UCLan). This degree unites practical workshop mechanics with formal circuit analysis, electromagnetic theory, power systems, sensor instrumentation, and digital electronics.

In parallel, I have built an extensive background in Computer Science & Information Communications Technology (ICT)—developing an in-depth understanding of system architectures, low-level computing, networking, and digital tooling. Additionally, my background as a former DJ developed my practical understanding of live sound engineering, signal routing, audio telemetry, and high-pressure crowd performance.
