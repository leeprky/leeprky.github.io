// 03.1 BUILD PLAN: SUB-£15 CAR ROADMAP

4-Phase Revival Schedule

PHASE 01 // SOURCING & INITIAL TRIAGE

Acquire the vehicle for under £15 initial outlay. Inspect chassis, check engine turnover by hand, and log all mechanical and electrical faults.

PHASE 02 // 12V ELECTRICAL & IGNITION

Test battery health, starter motor feed, alternator charging output, earth straps, fuses, and sensor connectors.

PHASE 03 // FLUIDS, BRAKES & SERVICING

Fresh oil & filter, coolant flush, hydraulic brake line bleeding, and freeing up seized calipers or linkages.

PHASE 04 // SHAKEDOWN & DOCUMENTATION

Full test run, cost breakdown sheet, and before/after photo log published right here in the gallery.

How My Themes Are Built

My open-source Discord themes (WindowsXI, MaterialYou, Vintage, and Lode) use modular CSS custom properties so users can tweak colours, blur levels, and spacing easily:

  --win11-acrylic-bg: rgba(16, 24, 39, 0.72);
  --win11-blur-radius: 18px;
  --win11-corner-radius: 8px;
  --accent-primary: #38bdf8;
}
