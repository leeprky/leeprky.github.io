📜 UNO Pub Crawl Rules & Points

⚙️ SETUP & ESSENTIALS

  • • Teams: Red 🔴, Blue 🔵, Green 🟢, Yellow 🟡
  • • Deck Setup: Remove all Wild cards before dealing. Deal 5 cards to each team's Captain, then shuffle all Wild cards back into the draw deck.
  • • Hand Size: Each team starts with 5 non-Wild cards in hand.
  • • Cross-Scoring:
    🔴 Red tracks 🔵 Blue
    🔵 Blue tracks 🟢 Green
    🟢 Green tracks 🟡 Yellow
    🟡 Yellow tracks 🔴 Red

🎲 STANDARD PLAY

  • • Turn Rules: On your team's turn, play 1 card that matches the top discard by COLOR or NUMBER. If you can't play, draw 1 card. If WILD, place into Captain's deck and draw another regular card.
  • • Captain's Stash: Captains keep their hand secret, with up to 2 face-down WILD cards hidden from opposing teams.

🃏 CARD TRIGGERS

  • 0 (Rule Maker): Create ANY rule for the current pub.
  • 7 (Wild Thief): Steal 1 Wild card from a selected team's stash! (Target team takes 2 sips UNLESS opposing team has none).
  • SKIP: Skips next team and gives them 1 sip each.
  • DRAW TWO: Target team takes 2 sips (Stacks to +4).
  • WILD (REVERSE): Add to Captain's stash to reverse an incoming attack.
  • WILD: Target a rival team for a special event (Spin the Wheel of Doom!).
  • WILD (+4): Every member of target team downs drink or takes a shot.

📜 POINTS SYSTEM

  • 7 (Wild Thief): Robbed team (or Defender if Reversed) -2 Pts
  • SKIP: To Skipped Team -1 Pt
  • DRAW TWO: Target (-2 or -4 stacked) OR Defender (+2/+4 if Reversed) ±2 / ±4
  • WILD (REVERSE): To Attacking Team -4 Pts
  • WILD (WHEEL): Winner +6 / Loser -6 +6 / -6
  • WILD (+4): Opposing -4 & Attacker +4 +4 / -4
  • Spewing: Tactical or otherwise -5 Pts
  • Rule Violation: Breaking pub/game rules -1 Pt
  • Holding Wilds in Captain's Deck at End of Round: +1 Ea (Max +2)
