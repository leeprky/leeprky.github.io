📜 OFFICIAL DRUNK UNO RULES & SETUP ESSENTIALS

⚓ SETUP & ESSENTIALS

  • Teams: Red 🔴, Blue 🔵, Green 🟢, Yellow 🟡.
  • Deck Setup: Remove all Wild cards before dealing. Deal 5 cards to each team's Captain, then shuffle all Wild cards back into the draw deck.
  • Hand Size: Each team starts with 5 non-Wild cards in hand.
  • Cross-Scoring: Team Red tracks Blue, Blue tracks Green, Green tracks Yellow, Yellow tracks Red.

⚔️ STANDARD PLAY

  • On your team's turn, play 1 card that matches the top discard by COLOR or NUMBER. If you cannot play, draw 1 card. If WILD, place into Captain's deck and draw another regular card.
  • Captain's Stash: Captains keep their hand secret, with up to 2 face-down WILD cards hidden from opposing teams.

🃏 CARD TRIGGERS & DRINK ACTIONS

  • 0 (Rule Maker): Create ANY rule for the current pub (e.g. left hand drinking only).
  • 7 (Wild Thief): Steal 1 Wild card from a selected team's stash! (Target team takes 2 sips UNLESS opposing team has none).
  • SKIP: Skips next team and gives them 1 sip each.
  • DRAW TWO (+2): Target team takes 2 sips (Stacks To +4!).
  • WILD (REVERSE): Add to captain's stash to reverse an incoming attack.
  • WILD (WHEEL): Target a rival team for a special event on the Wheel of Doom!
  • WILD (+4): Every member of target team downs drink or takes a shot.
