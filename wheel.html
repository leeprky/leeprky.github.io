WHEEL OF DOOM

RED — Drink Swapper:

Swap your current drink with another team’s drink!

YELLOW — Drinker’s Freeze:

Stops all drinking penalties for that targeted team until their next turn!

YELLOW — Immunity Shield:

While frozen, a targeted crew cannot lose points or be forced to drink until their next turn begins!

Hold SPIN to charge power, or click for a quick spin!

BLUE — Shot Roulette:

Nominate 1 rival player to take a mystery sip/shot!

BLUE — Mystery Chalice:

Roll the tavern’s 6-vial roulette to decide which mystery concoction your nominated rival must drink!

GREEN — Chug Challenge:

5-second head-to-head chug race vs the attacker!

Scoring: Attacker gets +6 pts for a win / successful attack (victim gets -6 pts).
(Note: Drinker’s Freeze carries no point gain/loss).
